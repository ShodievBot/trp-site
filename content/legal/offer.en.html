2. Subject of the Agreement

2.1. Under this Offer, the Contractor undertakes, at the Customer's request, to render a package of services for preparing the Work for publication and securing its publication, and the Customer undertakes to accept the Services and pay for them under the terms of this Offer and the Order Form.

2.2. Depending on the package chosen by the Customer as specified in the Form, the Services include:

  • 2.2.1. consulting on the choice of Publication, editorial requirements, stages and terms of the publication process;

  • 2.2.2. selecting and recommending a Publication (journal) matching the subject matter and level of the Work;

  • 2.2.3. preparing the Work for publication: literary, scientific and technical editing, translation, formatting and adaptation to the requirements of the chosen Publication, aligning the metadata (abstract, keywords, author details, affiliations, ORCID) with the Publication's requirements;

  • 2.2.4. checking the Work for originality and consulting on increasing it;

  • 2.2.5. submitting (submission) the Work to the Publication's editorial office through the official manuscript submission systems;

  • 2.2.6. conducting all official correspondence with the Publication's editorial office on behalf of the Contractor, accompanying the peer review process, coordinating revisions in response to reviewers' comments;

  • 2.2.7. monitoring the fact of publication, obtaining the publication and its details (DOI, imprint data, link);

  • 2.2.8. monitoring the inclusion of the published Work in international databases (where applicable to the specific Publication);

  • 2.2.9. informing the Customer about the progress of the Services at the key stages.

2.3. The Contractor is not the editorial office of the Publication and does not decide whether the Work is accepted or rejected for publication: such a decision is made exclusively by the Publication's editorial office. The Contractor performs organisational, preparatory, technical and representative actions aimed at preparing the Work for publication and securing its submission and publication.

2.4. The specific terms of the Services for each Work (the Publication or package, subject matter, list of authors, timelines, price) are fixed in the Order Form completed and signed by the Customer. The Form together with the Offer constitutes a single Agreement and replaces the conclusion of a separate written agreement (Section 15).

2.5. The Contractor does not guarantee the acceptance of the Work by the Publication's editorial office, as this decision is made solely by the editorial office (clause 2.3). The consequences of rejection and the refund terms are set out in Section 7.

4.2. 4.3. The timelines for the Services are specified in the Order Form. 7.1. Guaranteed refund (100%). If the Work is not published within the agreed time limit (including the extensions under clause 4.3 and the cases of change of Publication under clause 7.5) for reasons beyond the Customer's control, and the Customer has fully performed its obligations under the Agreement, the Contractor refunds the amount paid in full within 30 (thirty) calendar days from the moment the circumstances giving rise to the refund arise. No penalties, fees or deductions are applied to the refunded amount.

7.2. Refund procedure. The refund is made on the basis of the Customer's written (including electronic) application by non-cash transfer to the account (card) indicated by the Customer in the application or in the Form.

7.3. Customer's withdrawal. If the Customer, on its own initiative, withdraws from the Services or from the publication before the expiry of the agreed publication timeline (whether or not the Work has already been submitted to the Publication), the amount paid is not refundable and is retained by the Contractor as payment for the Services actually rendered (selection of the Publication, preparation and editing of the Work, administrative expenses, correspondence and accompaniment), since such Services cannot be returned.

7.4. Circumstances equated to the Customer's unilateral withdrawal (no refund is made):

  • 7.4.1. termination of communication with the Contractor without cause, failure to respond to requests, hindering the provision of the Services;

  • 7.4.2. refusal to provide the necessary materials, information, documents or revisions;

  • 7.4.3. breach of the obligations established in clause 6.3;

  • 7.4.4. independent submission of the Work or its parallel transfer to another intermediary;

  • 7.4.5. discovery of plagiarism, falsified data or copyright infringement in the Work, resulting in its rejection or removal from publication.

7.5. Rejection by the Publication. The parties acknowledge that rejection of the Work by an editorial office on editorial or academic grounds is not a breach committed by the Contractor. In such a case, within the agreed overall timeline, the Contractor submits the Work to an alternative Publication of a comparable level. If, within the agreed time limit (including extensions), publication has not taken place in any Publication, the provisions of clause 7.1 apply.

7.6. A refund is made only in the cases expressly provided for in this Section. In all other cases, the amounts paid remain with the Contractor as payment for the Services actually rendered, on a lawful basis.

7.7. Regardless of the ground, the obligation to refund is deemed performed on the date the funds are debited from the Contractor's account.
